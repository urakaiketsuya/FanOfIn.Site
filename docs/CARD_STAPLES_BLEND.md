# Blended card staples

The blended source pools full-archive tournament entries and unique community lists.
Each source observation has equal weight; this is not a 50/50 average of source rates.
A list appearing in both sources can contribute to both populations because the
published projections do not expose cross-source deck identities.

Deck counts and total copies are summed after remapping card dictionaries by ID.
Usage divides the combined card count by the combined matching-section population.
Card-presence patterns preserve the union of matching cards within each source;
minimum sample size and sorting do not change this denominator. Missing pattern
metadata makes filtered percentages unavailable rather than guessing.

Only full-archive cohorts are combined because community dates are not reliable.
Win rates and their sample counts remain tournament-only. Average copies uses pooled
copies divided by pooled deck counts. Typical copies is omitted because published
source modes cannot reconstruct a combined quantity histogram. Both sources must
load before the blend is shown; each retains its own refresh and retry status.
